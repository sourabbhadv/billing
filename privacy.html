Privacy Policy

ezy Billing and Accounting · Last updated: 28 November 2026

ezy Billing and Accounting is a billing and accounting app for shops. This page explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. "We" means the developer of the app (contact details at the bottom).

In short: your bills, parties, items and stock are kept on your device and in your own Google Drive. We do not keep a copy of them on our servers. Some features (the AI assistant and WhatsApp messaging) send part of that data to other services when you choose to use them. Details are below.

Information stored on your device

Bills, parties (customers and suppliers), items, stock, balances, payments and settings are stored in your device's browser storage. This is your shop data. We cannot see it unless you use a feature below that sends it somewhere.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, received when you sign in with Google. We use them to identify your account and your plan.
  • Plan and payment records: which plan your account has, its dates, and the payment reference from Razorpay.

We do not collect your contacts, your location or your browsing activity, and the app does not show advertising.

Features that send data to other services

  • Google Drive backup and live sync. If you turn these on, the app asks for permission to create and manage only the files it creates in your Google Drive (the drive.file permission). It cannot see your other files. Backup files stay in your own Drive and are used to restore your shop data on your devices.
  • AI assistant. If you set up the assistant, you paste your own key from Google Gemini or Groq. When you ask a question, a summary of your shop data is sent to that provider so it can answer. This can include party names, balances, item names and prices, stock, and recent bills. The data is sent under your own key and is subject to that provider's terms and privacy policy. If you do not set up the assistant, nothing is sent. Your key is saved in your device's browser storage only and is not sent to us.
  • WhatsApp. The "WhatsApp" buttons open WhatsApp on your device with a message already filled in, using the party's phone number you entered.
  • Payments. Plan purchases are handled by Razorpay. Card, UPI and bank details are entered with Razorpay and are never seen or stored by us.

Other services we use

  • Supabase stores your sign-in account, plan records.
  • Google Sign-In signs you in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app, and Google Fonts and public script hosts (Cloudflare cdnjs, jsDelivr) deliver fonts and code libraries. Like any website, these may see your IP address when files load.

Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to run sign-in and your backups. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services listed above, and only as needed to run sign-in, payments, backup, the AI assistant and WhatsApp when you use them. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep information

Account and plan records are kept while your account is active. After you delete your account, we remove them, except payment records we must keep for accounting and legal reasons. Shop data on your device and backups in your Google Drive stay under your control, and you can delete them at any time.

Security

Connections to our servers and to the services above use HTTPS. The optional passcode and PINs in the app are a convenience lock for a shared device and are not encryption. Keep your phone and Google account secure, and keep your own backups.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from inside the app.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete backup files from your own Google Drive.
  • Delete your account and records: see Delete your account.

Children

The app is built for shop owners and staff and is not meant for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
