Privacy Policy

Billing App · https://sourabbhadv.github.io/billing/
Last updated: 18 November 2026

This app is a billing tool for tile shops. This page explains what information it uses and why.

Your shop data stays on your device

Bills, parties, items, stock and balances are stored on the device you use. We do not upload them to our servers and we cannot see them.

What we collect

  • Google account details: your name and email address, received when you sign in with Google. We use them to identify your account and your Pro licence.
  • Licence and payment status: whether your account has Pro, and the payment reference from Razorpay.

We do not collect your contacts, location, or browsing activity.

Google Drive backup

If you turn on backup or live sync, the app asks for permission to create and manage only the files it creates itself in your Google Drive. It cannot see your other Drive files. Backup files go to your own Drive and are used only to restore your shop data on your devices.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores your sign-in account and Pro licence.
  • Razorpay processes payments. Card, UPI and bank details are entered on Razorpay and are never seen or stored by us.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the services above, and only as needed to run sign-in, payments and backup.

Your choices

  • Sign out at any time from inside the app.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete backup files from your own Google Drive at any time.
  • To delete your account and licence record, email us at the address below.
