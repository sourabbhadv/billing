Delete your account

ezy Billing and Accounting · Last updated: 28 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your ezy Billing and Accounting account and the records we hold about it.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the Google email address you use to sign in to the app.
  2. Use the subject Delete my account.
  3. We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (name and email).
  • Your plan and licence record.
  • Your WhatsApp wallet balance and its records. Use any remaining balance before you ask, because it cannot be refunded after deletion unless the law requires it.

What we may keep

Payment records (such as the Razorpay payment reference and amount) may be kept for as long as accounting, tax or legal rules require. They are not used for anything else.

What stays under your control

  • Data on your device. Your bills, parties, items and stock are stored on your device. Clear the app's data in your browser or device settings to remove them.
  • Backups in Google Drive. Backup files are in your own Google Drive. Delete them there, then remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • AI provider data. If you used the AI assistant, your Gemini or Groq account is separate from ours. Manage it with that provider.

Deletion cannot be undone. Export or back up anything you want to keep before you ask.
